Privacy Policy

FoneSquare ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Data We Collect
We collect personal information you provide during registration: name, phone number, email, nationality, identity documents for KYC compliance.

2. How We Use Your Data
Account creation and login · Identity verification and sanctions screening · Order processing and delivery · Customer support · Compliance with legal obligations.

3. Data Sharing
We share data only with: JD Cloud (SMS delivery), WhatsApp Business API (OTP), risk control partners (sanctions screening), and as required by law.

4. Data Retention
Account data is retained for the life of your account + 3 years. KYC documents are retained per regulatory requirements (minimum 5 years).

5. Your Rights
You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data. Contact: privacy@fonesquare.com
